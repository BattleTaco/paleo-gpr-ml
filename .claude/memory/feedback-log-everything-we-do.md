For paleo-gpr-ml, **note down everything and anything we do** — the *process*, not just the findings. Every step, decision, experiment, dead end, install, parameter choice, and "why we did it this way" gets written down so Michael (and any agent) can look back on the process and move forward correctly.

**Why:** Michael and I are working as partners to become real ML *researchers* in the paleontology space. A reproducible, honest paper trail of how we got somewhere is as important as the result — it's how we learn, defend the work, and avoid repeating mistakes. This is for his learning as much as the research. See [[reference-sources-library]].

**How to apply:**
- After any meaningful action (new script, experiment run, design decision, blocker hit), record it in the right place: a research note in `docs/notes/0X_*.md`, the relevant `docs/*.md` plan/TODO, or his Obsidian vault — in his voice (see [[feedback-write-in-michaels-voice]]).
- Capture decisions AND the reasoning/alternatives considered, not just the outcome. Note what's blocked and why.
- Keep `docs/planning/next_steps_todo.md` current as work completes.
- Default to over-documenting; a process we can replay beats a result we can't explain. See [[research-priorities-paleo-gpr]].
